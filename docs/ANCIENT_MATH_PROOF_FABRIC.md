and `szl-khipu` (integrity kernel). It is **not** a live eight-organ deployment.

mathematical witness, requiring identical tenant identity and both sub-records
at source-only *review* states. Both SHA-256s are **unsigned content checksums**.
The result **never** authorizes an effect, source promotion, training, a database
write, deployment or a Hatun tool call. It does not modify any canonical organ.

## Numeric conservation tests and why they matter

The proposed **cross-representation arithmetic witness** requires:

\[
N = \sum_{i=0}^{k-1} d_i 10^{k-1-i}
  = \sum_{j=0}^{m-1} s_j 60^{m-1-j},
\quad 0\le d_i < 10,\quad 0\le s_j < 60.
\]

It independently verifies `EgyptianDoubling(N,b) = Nb` and the division chain
`a_i = q_i a_{i+1}+a_{i+2}` for Euclidean GCD, computes a bounded unit-fraction
identity `p/q = Σ 1/d_i`, and verifies `LCM(260,365)=18,980`. These are
**modern exact-arithmetic checks inspired by different traditions**, not new
theorems or historical evidence that those cultures exchanged algorithms.

The mathematical differentiation is *admission hygiene*: a multi-source
agent's claim of a common quantity is refused if the independently encoded
values disagree, the corpus is not synthetic, an arithmetic operation exceeds
its bound, the tenant differs, the organ contract holds, or provenance is
incomplete. A high `Λ` is never authority. This lowers opportunities for
accidental or malicious cross-source conflation but has **no measured
false-green-rate or production performance claim yet**.

### Source-only admission states

- `NUMERIC_ANALOGY_READY_FOR_REVIEW`: all synthetic arithmetic identities check.
- `HOLD`: unreviewed physical artifact, cross-notation mismatch, unbounded
  fraction, zero-composition or any other declared refusal.
- `JOINT_PROPOSAL_READY_FOR_OWNER_REVIEW`: both source-only junctions provided
  reviewable evidence for the **same** tenant. All effect-related fields are
  still false or zero, and no cryptographic receipt was signed.

A caller can rewrite and rehash an **unsigned** JSON record. Therefore
`verify_heritage_witness()` and `verify_joint_advisory()` only detect accidental
changes relative to the presented checksum **and cannot authenticate origin**.
Never treat either check as a transaction, licence, independent source readback,
policy approval or permissions certificate.
